PRIVACY

Your browser data, explained.

Plain-language privacy notes for this static demonstration.

Local browser storage

SmartCart stores your cart, wishlist and budget in localStorage on the device and browser you are using. This demonstration does not send these values to a SmartCart server. You can clear them using your browser's site-data controls.

Contact and checkout forms

The checkout and contact forms are local demonstrations. They do not transmit, retain or process the values you type. Do not enter sensitive personal or payment information.

External images

Product photography is loaded from Unsplash image URLs. When those images load, your browser requests them from that image host, which may receive normal request information such as your IP address. Images are illustrative rather than exact product depictions.

Changes

This is a student project and this notice describes the current demo behavior. It is not legal advice.
