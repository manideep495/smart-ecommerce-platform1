ABOUT THE PROJECT

Shopping, with a little more clarity.

SmartCart is a static college demonstration project for everyday product discovery.

What SmartCart does

Browse a small sample catalog, save products to a wishlist, manage a cart, set a spending limit and compare product details. Each of these features runs in your browser.

How suggestions are made

Recommendations use fixed, visible rules: products already in the cart are excluded; products under the saved budget are prioritized; items in the same category as the first cart product receive a complementary priority; illustrative rating and price then sort the remaining options. There is no AI or machine learning.

Sample product information

Product details, prices, ratings, review counts, features and warranty periods are demonstration data. They are not current offers or verified commercial specifications. Product images are illustrative and may not depict the exact sample product.

Checkout

Checkout is simulated. This site does not take payment, send an order to a retailer, or provide real shipping or inventory information.
