HOW THE RULES WORK

Transparent by design.

  1. Products already in your cart are left out of suggestions.
  2. A product within your saved budget gets priority.
  3. Products in the same category as your first cart item get a complementary-product priority.
  4. Higher illustrative rating breaks remaining ties; price then provides a stable ordering.

The same explicit rules run in your browser. SmartCart does not use AI or machine learning.
